Each announcement will set out the coach, date, venue, format, experience requirements and price.

I'm new to grappling. Can I attend?

Check the experience requirements for the specific event. If you are unsure, tell us how long you have trained and ask before booking.

I'm competing or coaching. What should I look for?

Look at the topic and session format. Choose material that fits the positions you are working on, then check any partner or equipment requirements.

What if I need an adjustment to take part?

Contact us before booking about access, communication or participation needs. We can discuss what the particular event and venue can support.

Does joining the list reserve a place?

No. The list is for event updates. Tickets, availability and booking conditions will be explained when bookings open.
