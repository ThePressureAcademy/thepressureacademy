Sample lesson · Seated guard

One position.
Three decisions.

Start in seated butterfly guard, facing a kneeling partner. The partner chooses one of the reactions below. The seated player reads the change and responds.

Open the sneak preview (PDF)
  1. 16

    They commit weight over one hook.

    Butterfly sweep

    Come on top and settle before resetting.

  2. 17

    They back away far enough to stand safely.

    Technical stand-up

    Finish on your feet, balanced and facing your partner.

  3. 18

    They turn the corner and catch one leg in half guard.

    Knee-shield recovery

    Recover the knee shield and square up to your partner.

Watch their first decision

Switch roles after three starts. Count a rep when the player chooses the response that fits the feed and finishes under control. Ask which change they noticed.
