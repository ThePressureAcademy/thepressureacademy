Before you decide

Do we need the assessment before monthly support?

Yes. The assessment produces the Teaching Blueprint that Kirsty uses for the monthly Pathway. You can also use your blueprint independently.

How long do we commit to the Pathway?

It is month-to-month. Your family can cancel when you are ready to continue independently.

Are individual sessions included?

A targeted 1:1 session is an optional, separately priced add-on. Kirsty will discuss it with you if a specific need comes up.
