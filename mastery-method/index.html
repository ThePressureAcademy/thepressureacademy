From a spoken story to a first sentence

An example from Year 2 writing shows what your child’s teacher can add to the scorecard.

See the sample Teaching Blueprint
The homework
Retell a story in three sentences, one each for the beginning, middle and end.
What the teacher notices
The child can retell all three parts aloud. When the pencil comes out, they need help starting their first sentence.
What to try at home
Draw three boxes. Ask your child to say one sentence for each box, then write the first sentence in their own words.
