Pressure Tested · First drop

Toa

Warrior, in te reo Māori.

I built Pressure Tested because I’ve lived the last two and a half years under pressure after a workplace incident. Some days, getting through the day is the fight.

Beneath the lettering on the back of the hoodie is a Māori warrior wearing moko. I named our first drop Toa, the Māori word for warrior. To me, that face stands for what it’s taken to keep going.

Cody · Founder, Pressure Tested
