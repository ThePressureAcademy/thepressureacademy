Measure a piece you wear

Lay a tee or hoodie with your preferred fit on a flat surface. Smooth the fabric without stretching it. For a cropped cut, use a garment that ends where you want yours to sit.

Half chest
Measure straight across from one underarm to the other. Record the flat width in centimetres.
Body length
Start at the shoulder beside the neck and measure straight down to the bottom hem. Include the hem band and leave the hood out.
Shoulder and sleeve
Measure across the back between the two shoulder seams. For sleeve length, measure from the shoulder seam to the end of the sleeve.
